Sporadic M₁₂

A puzzle on the Mathieu group M₁₂.

Twelve coloured marbles sit in a ring. Spin the ring and swap the paired marbles to scramble them — then find your way back home. Record a sequence as a macro and replay it (or its inverse) to crack the harder arrangements. Choose from 341 swap permutations for a different puzzle each time.
