M12

The Basics

M12, by Sporadic Games, is a permutation puzzle (sometimes known as a "combination puzzle").

The goal of the puzzle is to return to the Home position (shown on the right) starting from a random puzzle, by using the Left, Swap and Right moves. In the Home position the number over each ball matches the small gray number next to the ball. M12 puzzlers often refer to the top ball as the "0-ball", the one underneath it as the "1-ball", and so on.

Right and Left are opposites of one another, moving the circle of numbers clockwise and counter-clockwise around the ring of 23 balls, leaving the number on the top ball unchanged.

Swap exchanges all the pairs of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color, including exchanging the number on the top white ball with the one on the white ball directly below it.

The Left, Swap and Right buttons in the middle of the ring make the corresponding moves. In addition, you can spin the ring by dragging it with your finger, and you can Swap by flicking the top ball downwards.

Getting Started

M12 will make a random puzzle for you to solve if you shake your iPhone or iPod, or if you hit the Shake button.

Solving the Puzzle

Each random puzzle is generated by a long sequence of random moves, so it is guaranteed to be solvable ... but that doesn't mean it will be easy!

Puzzlers often find a sequence of moves that, when the sequence is complete, have made some useful changes while leaving other balls undisturbed. More precisely, the numbers on those "fixed" balls are moved away during the sequence, but moved back by the end. These "macro" moves can be saved into the macro buttons A through E (see below) and then invoked with a single button tap.
