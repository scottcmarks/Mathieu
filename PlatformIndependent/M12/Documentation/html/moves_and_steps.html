Moves and Steps

A step is the smallest recorded change -- a single tick left or right, or a swap.

A move is a larger recorded change, differing only from a step in that a series of left or right ticks are treated as a single move.

A combo move is counted as a single move but as the total number steps it uses.

The pair of numbers to the left of the history show the number of moves (upper) and steps (lower) you have made since the last Home or Shake.
