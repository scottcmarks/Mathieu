<h2 style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 14.0px 0.0px; font: 18.0px Times; min-height: 23.0px"><b></b><br></h2>
<p class="p2">Spin and swap!<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>The M12 puzzle is a cousin of Rubik's cube and the "fifteen" puzzle.<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>It<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>is a puzzle you can start working on immediately -- it's so simple!<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>Spin the ring of numbers around with your finger, or swap all the pairs of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color by flicking down from the "0-ball.<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>Or, if it's more your style,<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>just use the buttons in the middle of the ring. <span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p3"><br></p>
<p class="p2">The numbers start out all mixed up, and your job is to return them to the "Home position" where the number over each ball matches the small gray number next to the ball.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p3"><br></p>
<p class="p2">So easy ... and yet ...</p>
<p class="p3"><br></p>
<p class="p2">The game generates its puzzle the same way you will solve it - by either spinning the ring of numbers or doing a "swap," exchanging all the pairs of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color. <span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>Each random puzzle is generated by a long sequence of random moves, so it is guaranteed to be solvable ... but that doesn't mean it will be easy! </p>
<p class="p3"><br></p>
<p class="p2">Like the Rubik's cube, there are moves you will want to repeat to solve this puzzle.<span class="Apple-converted-space">  </span>M12 helps you out with combo buttons (2 on the free version, 5 on the paid version) that work like presets on your radio -- just press them 'til they "beep" and they capture your favorite move!</p>
<p class="p3"><br></p>
<p class="p4">These help notes refer mostly to the full version, with notes where M12 Free is different.</p>
<h3 style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 14.0px 0.0px; font: 14.0px Times"><b>The Moves</b></h3>
<p class="p2"><span class="s1"><a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/Images/left_position.png">Left</a></span> and <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/Images/right_position.png"><span class="s1">Right</span></a> are opposites of one another, moving the circle of numbers one spot around the ring of 11 balls, but leaving the number on the top ball unchanged.</p>
<p class="p4"><span class="s1"><a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/Images/swap_position.png">Swap</a></span> exchanges all the pairs of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color.</p>
<p class="p4">The <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/buttons.html#left"><span class="s1">Left</span></a>, <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/buttons.html#swap"><span class="s1">Swap</span></a> and <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/buttons.html#right"><span class="s1">Right</span></a> buttons in the middle of the ring make the corresponding moves.  You can also spin the ring by dragging it with your finger, and you can Swap by flicking the top ball downwards.</p>
<h3 style="margin: 0.0px 0.0px 14.0px 0.0px; font: 14.0px Times"><b>Getting Started</b></h3>
<p class="p4">M12 will make a random puzzle for you to solve if you shake your iPhone or iPod, or if you hit the <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/buttons.html#shake"><span class="s1">Shake</span></a> button.  All the buttons are described in more detail <a href="file:///iPhoneSandbox/Mathieu/M12/PlatformIndependent/Documentation/buttons.html"><span class="s1">here</span></a>.</p>
