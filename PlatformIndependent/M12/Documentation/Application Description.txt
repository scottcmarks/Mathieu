
    *Sporadic M12*

Spin and swap!  The M12 puzzle is a cousin of Rubik's cube and the
"fifteen" puzzle.  It  is a puzzle you can start working on immediately
-- it's so simple!  Spin the ring of numbers around with your finger, or
swap all the pairs of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color
by flicking down from the "0-ball.  Or, if it's more your style,  just
use the buttons in the middle of the ring.  


The numbers start out all mixed up, and your job is to return them to
the "Home position" where the number over each ball matches the small
gray number next to the ball. 


So easy ... and yet ...


The game generates its puzzle the same way you will solve it - by either
spinning the ring of numbers or doing a "swap," exchanging all the pairs
of numbers over the pairs of balls of the same color.   Each random
puzzle is generated by a long sequence of random moves, so it is
guaranteed to be solvable ... but that doesn't mean it will be easy! 


Like the Rubik's cube, there are moves you will want to repeat to solve
this puzzle.  M12 helps you out with combo buttons (2 on the free
version, 5 on the paid version) that work like presets on your radio --
just press them 'til they "beep" and they capture your favorite move!


      *Getting Started*

M12 will make a random puzzle for you to solve if you shake your iPhone
or iPod, or if you hit the Shake button.
